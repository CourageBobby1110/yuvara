# YuVara Design Principles: Ultra-Minimal, Quiet & Human E-Commerce

## 1. Product-First (Instant Shopping)
- Shoppers land on the store to browse and buy products, not to read marketing essays or introductory paragraphs.
- Put real products front-and-center immediately at the top of the fold.
- Avoid large filler text, long manifestos, or marketing essays.

## 2. Zero Icon Bloat & Zero Artificial Clutter
- Never add unnecessary decorative icons (e.g., locks, clocks, stars, shields, medals, badges).
- No artificial urgency meters, countdown clock widgets, or heavy progress tracks unless explicitly requested.
- Rely on typography, white space, and subtle lines for visual hierarchy.

## 3. Quiet, Airy, Human Styling
- Simple, understated header rows (e.g., quiet uppercase eyebrow on the left, clean `View all →` on the right).
- Frameless product cards:
  - 4:5 image aspect ratio.
  - Soft corner radius (`12px` to `14px`).
  - Subtle neutral photo backdrops (`#f5f3ef`).
  - Gentle micro-interactions on hover (e.g. `transform: translateY(-2px)`, subtle shadow `0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`).
- Restrained frosted pill badges for discounts (e.g., `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`).
- Clean, single-line truncated product names with tabular numeric prices.

## 4. Typography & Readability
- Use clean, modern **Inter** (`var(--font-inter)`).
- Maintain crisp contrast, natural font weights (550 to 700), and comfortable letter-spacing.
- Keep mobile layouts calm, generous, and touch-friendly (2-column grids on mobile/tablet, 4-column on desktop).
