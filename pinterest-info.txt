2. READY-TO-PASTE FORM FIELDS FOR UPGRADE APPLICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
When you click "Upgrade to Standard Access" in the Pinterest Developer Portal,
use these exact details:

App Name:
YuVara Store Catalog & Pinning

Use Case Description:
"YuVara is an online e-commerce fashion and lifestyle retail store. We use the 
Pinterest API v5 to connect our store via official OAuth 2.0 and automatically 
publish and synchronize our curated product catalog directly as Pins to our 
official Pinterest boards. Each Pin includes live pricing, high-resolution 
product photography, rich product descriptions, and direct product checkout 
links back to our verified store."

Requested Scopes:
- boards:read
- boards:write
- pins:read
- pins:write
- user_accounts:read

Scope Justification:
- boards:read & boards:write: Allows the store owner to select or create 
  target boards to organize products by category.
- pins:read & pins:write: Enables publishing product Pins with title, description, 
  pricing, and direct checkout links.
- user_accounts:read: Identifies the authenticated store account and ensures 
  the admin is publishing to their verified business profile.

3. STEP-BY-STEP DEMO VIDEO RECORDING SCRIPT (60 TO 90 SECONDS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
*IMPORTANT: Reviewers will reject the video if the Pinterest OAuth consent 
 screen is skipped or cut out. Do not edit it out!*

[0:00 - 0:15] STEP 1: START IN YUVARA ADMIN
- Open your browser to https://yuvara.com.ng/admin/settings (or localhost:3000/admin/settings).
- Scroll down to the "Pinterest Integration & Auto-Pinning" card.
- Show the "Connect with Pinterest (OAuth 2.0)" button.

[0:15 - 0:35] STEP 2: SHOW THE PINTEREST OAUTH CONSENT SCREEN
- Click the red "Connect with Pinterest (OAuth 2.0)" button.
- Let the browser redirect to https://www.pinterest.com/oauth/...
- Make sure the permissions list (boards, pins, profile) is visible on screen.
- Click "Allow" / "Grant Access".

[0:35 - 0:50] STEP 3: RETURN TO ADMIN & VERIFY CONNECTION
- The browser redirects back to YuVara Admin Settings.
- Highlight the green "Pinterest Connected via OAuth 2.0" status and 
  the "@username Connected" banner.

[0:50 - 1:15] STEP 4: TRIGGER AN API PIN ACTION
- Click "Load My Boards" to fetch boards via the API.
- Select your target board.
- Click "Sync Products to Pinterest".
- Wait for the success confirmation toast ("Sync completed").

[1:15 - 1:30] STEP 5: VERIFY LIVE ON PINTEREST
- Switch to a new tab and open your Pinterest profile (pinterest.com/your-username).
- Navigate to the board and show the freshly created Pin live with its product image, 
  title, price, and clickable link back to yuvara.com.ng.
- End the recording.
